## Email the site maintainer

Email: [support@deckcraft.me](mailto:support@deckcraft.me)

Use this address for website questions, inaccurate instructions, broken links, accessibility feedback, and privacy-related requests. You can copy the address into your email application or use the email link above; no account or contact form is required.

Response times vary with the independent maintainer's availability; a reply within a fixed number of days is not guaranteed.

## What to include

For a content correction, include the page URL and the passage that needs attention. For an editing problem, describe your operating system, browser or application version, what you expected, and what actually happened. Mention whether you used the hosted browser editor or a separately downloaded desktop application.

Start with a short description or a nonconfidential screenshot. Do not send passwords, account credentials, private customer information, or the only copy of an important presentation. If a document is necessary to reproduce an issue, first make a small example that removes sensitive material.

## Website support and upstream software

DeckCraft.me is an independent guide website. The maintainer can review issues with this site and its content, but cannot promise fixes to the upstream application. Application bugs can also be checked against the [upstream issue tracker](https://github.com/storytold/deckcraft/issues).

Learn [who maintains the site and how the guides are prepared](/about). The [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy) explains the treatment of information you choose to send.
