## About this site

DeckCraft at deckcraft.me is an independent tool and guide website offering English download instructions, direct access to the online editor, beginner tutorials, online trial links, and comparisons of the differences and advantages relative to Microsoft PowerPoint. The upstream software is developed by ArtCraft and its contributors. This page describes this website; external websites and downloaded applications have their own practices.

## Your presentations

The browser editor processes presentation files on your device. This website does not provide cloud document storage or upload presentation contents to an account database. Save or export a copy to your device before closing the editor. Do not rely on a browser session as your only copy.

If you send an email to support@deckcraft.me or include presentation text or attachments in a support ticket, the message, contact details and attachments are submitted for handling your request. Avoid sending sensitive documents when a brief description is enough.

## Optional accounts and support

Reading guides and opening the browser editor do not require a site account. If you choose to create an account, we process the details you provide, authentication records and sessions. Account and support records are stored using the website’s Cloudflare D1 database. Authentication cookies help keep signed-in sessions working.

Support tickets contain the message, account information and any attachments you choose to submit. For website, guide, online access, account or privacy questions, email [support@deckcraft.me](mailto:support@deckcraft.me). Signed-in users can also use [Settings → Support Tickets](/settings/tickets).

## Site operation and cookies

Hosting services may process request information such as IP address, requested URL, browser details and timestamps to deliver the site, diagnose errors and prevent abuse. Technical request information is separate from presentation contents processed inside the editor.

A first-touch referral cookie or local browser record may remember how you first reached this website. It is used for site referral attribution, not to inspect your presentations. Locale preferences and authentication may also use cookies. You can clear cookies and site data in your browser; doing so may sign you out or reset preferences.

Optional analytics depend on the website’s configuration. We do not claim that an analytics integration is enabled merely because the site supports it. If one is enabled, its use should be disclosed here. We do not sell presentation contents.

## External links

Official downloads are served by GitHub, and links to ArtCraft and Microsoft open their websites. Those services may receive request information when you visit or download from them and apply their own privacy terms.

## Retention and your choices

Keep your own local copies of presentations. Account records, support messages and technical logs are kept as needed for site operation, security and handling requests; this page does not promise a fixed deletion deadline. You can review available account settings, email [support@deckcraft.me](mailto:support@deckcraft.me), or submit a support ticket to request access, correction or deletion of account information. We may need to verify account ownership before acting.

For a bug in DeckCraft itself, use the [upstream issue tracker](https://github.com/storytold/deckcraft/issues). Public issue reports are visible to others, so remove private information before posting.

## Updates

We update this page when the website’s features or data practices change. The revision date above identifies the current version.
